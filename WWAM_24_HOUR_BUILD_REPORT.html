The operating contract

FROM SOURCE RECEIPT
TO EDITOR PACKET.

Every show can open this workspace. A rich source gets source-local opportunities; a source with zero eligible opportunities gets an honest eligibility receipt and nothing to route. The system never borrows a hotter moment just to make the desk look busy.

JULY 23 TRIAGE6 fast-lane items1 research thread7 quarantined items4 reference-only lineages

These are creator workflow views, not an arithmetic partition of the 23-candidate manifest.

  1. 01
    LOCK THE SHOW

    Resolve one canonical source, its registry fingerprint, coverage state, receipt keys, and exact timestamp bounds.

  2. 02
    SURFACE THE WORK

    Register source-local Shorts, supercut memberships, resurfacing pairs, research threads, and storyboards with excerpts and rationale.

  3. 03
    ROUTE, DO NOT AUTO-PUBLISH

    A human marks Keep, Hold, or Reject. Unreviewed stays visibly unreviewed; local decisions survive a reload.

  4. 04
    EXPORT THE RECEIPTS

    The editor packet separates Unreviewed, Kept, Held, Rejected, and Reference-only material and carries every local coordinate.

  5. 05
    FAIL CLOSED ON DRIFT

    If source proof changes, saved routing is held for review instead of being silently attached to a different evidence set.

Why “I can make that with AI” misses the point

THE MOAT IS
THE REFUSAL LOGIC.

A generated page can look impressive. The hard part refuses attractive nonsense, preserves identity through every feature, and discloses what the evidence cannot support.

The differentiator

IT KNOWS
WHEN TO SAY
“NOT PROVEN.”

Major artifacts carry source IDs, receipt keys, exact bounds, registry bindings, and deterministic fingerprints. Fingerprints detect change; they are not signatures or truth.

RIGHT VIDEO ≠ RIGHT MOMENT

A source can be relevant while its timestamp is not evidence about the question.

SAME UPLOAD ≠ INTERACTION

Two entities in one source does not prove they spoke together.

CHRONOLOGY ≠ ORIGIN

Earliest indexed is not automatically first use or true origin.

MACHINE CANDIDATE ≠ REVIEW

Archive Deep candidates stay quarantined instead of inflating proof.

CURATED ≠ CERTIFIED

A selected window does not authenticate an editor or diarize a speaker.

PRECISION ≠ SEMANTIC TRUTH

An exact window can still be irrelevant; eligibility comes first.

VIEWER COPY ≠ ARCHIVE FACT

Custom cut titles remain viewer-written and never enter Canon.

UNKNOWN BEATS PLAUSIBLE

Unsupported subjects return absence instead of a confident guess.
