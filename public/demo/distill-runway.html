WHAT THIS MACHINE MAY AND MAY NOT CLAIM

THE EVIDENCE
FIREWALL.

SAFE BEFORE CAPTIONS

Canonical source facts

  • Official source ID and URL
  • Title, upload date, runtime, and cached views
  • Acquisition priority and its exact formula
  • Known caption-path state and next action
SEALED BEFORE EVIDENCE

Everything inside the show

  • No episode recap from a title
  • No “best” or “funniest” moment without a receipt
  • No speaker, intent, character, or quote attribution
  • No automatic promotion, canon, or rights claim
THE PRODUCT MOAT

Visible incompleteness

A generic chatbot hides uncertainty behind plausible prose. This system turns uncertainty into a bounded work item, preserves its source identity, and knows exactly what evidence must arrive next.
