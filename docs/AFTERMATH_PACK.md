# The Aftermath Pack

The Aftermath Pack turns one exact Show Wiki into a source-bound creator review
desk. It joins that show's registered receipts, Creator Clip Lab memberships,
show-delta summary, reference lineages, and Cold Open Factory storyboards
without treating any machine suggestion as a creator decision.

It answers a practical question: after a show has been indexed, what could a
human editor review next, and what exact source evidence supports each option?

## Per-show experience

The desk remains locked to one canonical source ID and source fingerprint. It
shows:

- the show's distilled status, new indexed topics, strongest topic, funniest
  registered receipt, and exact playable source coordinates;
- a source-local opportunity queue made from Short candidates, supercut
  memberships, and resurfacing pairs;
- proposed edit windows, archival excerpts, suggested promotional copy,
  transparent scores, evidence level, contextual risk, and review status;
- related sources as supporting production context, never as proof about the
  selected show;
- reference-only recurring-bit lineages and generated cold-open storyboards in
  their own clearly separated sections; and
- an exact-source route into Creator Clip Lab for deeper inspection.

Public archival excerpts remain capped at 16 words. Suggested titles, hooks,
and captions remain labeled editorial copy rather than archival quotes.
Automatic captions do not establish a speaker.

The 23 opportunity total is exactly `13 + 6 + 4`. The four lineages are

those units separate prevents a visually impressive inventory from becoming an
inflated claim.

## Readiness is a review aid

Every production opportunity lands in one transparent lane:

- **Clip-ready** means a low-risk, human-curated candidate is ready for a human
  to inspect.
- **Fast-review** means a medium-risk, human-curated candidate needs a faster
  context check.
- **Archive expansion** means a source membership in a multi-source supercut or
  resurfacing concept.
- **Research** means the machine found a bounded lead that still needs human
  verification.
- **Quarantine** means the risk gate keeps the item out of a normal creator
  handoff.

These labels rank editorial usefulness. They do not predict virality,
performance, revenue, audience response, or publication fitness.

## Keep, Hold, and Reject

The three desk actions are deliberately local:

| Action | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Keep | Keep for creator review |
| Hold | Hold for more context |
| Reject | Reject from this local pack |

A note may explain the routing decision. None of the three actions grants
creator approval, rights clearance, promotion authority, or permission to
publish. A quarantined item stays in the risk-quarantined export lane even when
someone locally marks it Keep.

## Fingerprinted review and export

The pack binds the source, Source Dossier, archive, ChannelPack, Creator Clip
Lab, and available Cold Open Factory build. A saved review also binds the exact
opportunity pack and its decisions. Restore fails closed when the source,
registry, pack, or decision payload has drifted or been altered.

The editor handoff is available as bounded JSON and Markdown. It separates:

- kept items awaiting creator review;
- items held for surrounding context;
- risk-quarantined items;
- rejected opportunity IDs;
- unreviewed work;
- reference-only lineages; and
- separate cold-open storyboards.

Each included opportunity preserves its source coordinates, proposed editorial
window, evidence, risk, and reproducibility fingerprints. The fingerprint is an
integrity and reproducibility check, not an owner signature or authenticated
approval.

The packet contains no media files, downloaded audio or video, transcript
payload, speaker inference, rights clearance, creator approval, publication
action, or performance promise. A human still must watch surrounding context,
set final picture and audio bounds, verify meaning and any speaker credit, and
apply language, platform, rights, title, thumbnail, and final-publish review.

## Portable contract

The reusable layer owns source locking, receipt and artifact joins, readiness
lanes, decision semantics, fingerprint checks, export structure, and refusal
boundaries. A ChannelPack supplies the channel's formats, editorial vocabulary,
recurring entities, risk policy, and visible voice.

For WWAM, that means Shorts, horror-topic resurfacing, recurring-character
lineages, WWAM-specific humor, and cold-open concepts. A racing league could
use the same contract for exact-race highlight candidates, driver storylines,
season callbacks, and booth moments without inheriting any WWAM labels. The
portable value is the verified path from one source receipt to one human review
decision and one reproducible handoff.
