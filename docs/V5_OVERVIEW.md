# WWAM After Midnight V5

## One-sentence product

WWAM After Midnight is a source-linked memory layer that turns WWAM's back
catalog into a fan destination, a searchable lore system, and a reviewable
creator workbench.

It is not an official WWAM product, a transcript replacement, or a chatbot
pretending to know the hosts. The proof is the connection between a useful
answer and its official source. When the answer relies on a moment-level
receipt, that proof also includes the exact indexed second. Source-level
metadata and summaries remain labeled as source-level evidence rather than
masquerading as timestamped utterances.

## How to read the proof

The build deliberately separates five evidence states:

| Public label | What has actually been established | What has not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source metadata | The official upload, title, date, duration, observed views, or caption availability | A quote, topic claim, or speaker |
| Source-level derived summary | A deterministic synopsis or editorial description attached to the whole source | That a host spoke the summary at a particular second |
| Machine-surfaced timestamped receipt | The source link and timestamp resolve, and a bounded auto-caption fragment exists there | Human-confirmed wording, category, target, intent, or speaker |
| Editor-verified performance event | A human checked the source, timestamp, and immediate performance context | Clip-level speaker identity when the caption track is not diarized |
| Creator-certified claim | An authorized creator approved the specific claim | Anything beyond the scope of that approval |

The current build contains 847 machine-level and 25 editor-level receipts, but
no creator-certified receipts. “Zero invalid timestamps” means the indexed
source/time pairs resolve and sit within the known source duration. It does not
mean a human has certified all 872 excerpts or the claims derived from them.

The owner-supplied Mike/J mappings describe who performs each recurring
character generally. They do not turn an individual auto-caption event into a
speaker-diarized clip. That second claim remains locked until clip-level human
or creator verification exists.

## The three front doors

### Fan Experience

The archive becomes something to play:

- Tape Trivia builds deterministic five- or ten-round games from 872 playable
  receipts and never asks the player to identify an undiarized speaker.
- The Evidence Bag saves moments on the device and exports a source manifest.
- Red Band Roulette, WWAM UP IN YA, Personalized Descent, and soundbyte paths
  turn archive discovery into a repeatable session instead of a search result.

### Deep Dive

The archive becomes navigable memory:

- Ask WWAM scopes questions to titles, topics, source lanes, popularity, and
  follow-up context, then explains its evidence boundary.
- Lore Galaxy organizes 177 field-guide entries into 820 receipt-backed edges,
  19 constellations, and 51 indexed lineages.
- Take Time Machine and WWAM Court expose chronological signals and competing
  receipts without silently turning an inference into canon.
- Fresh 10, Popular 25, franchise reports, and tape autopsies preserve the
  channel-specific reason each source matters.

Lore Galaxy's 953 labeled graph receipt links include source entry points and
context records. That number is intentionally broader than the 872
editorial-moment receipts.

### Creator Proof

The archive becomes reviewable production inventory:

- Clip Lab offers 560 timestamped Short candidates across 71 sources, 32
  multi-source supercut bundles, and 21 then/now resurfacing opportunities.
- Every candidate keeps archival text separate from suggested promotional copy,
  shows a proposed edit window, assigns evidence and context risk, and exports a
  deterministic JSON handoff.
- Campaign selections preserve an exact, fingerprinted receipt/source ledger.
  A filtered supercut reloads as the same filtered package; missing or altered
  evidence fails closed instead of widening to its parent bundle.
- Live Aftermath and the Control Room surface what a newly indexed stream added,
  which old source may be relevant again, and what a human should review next.
- Trust / Canon Desk exposes source health, correction packets, character
  attribution firewalls, claim audits, and local contribution packets.

No feature downloads media, publishes a clip, clears rights, or guarantees
performance. It shortens discovery and editorial preparation; a creator or
editor still watches the surrounding tape and makes the decision.

## The defensible layer

The visual treatment is not the moat. The compounding asset is:

```text
official source
  → source metadata or source-level summary
  → bounded timestamped receipt when a moment-level claim is made
  → channel-specific entity and relationship
  → transparent derivation
  → human review or correction
  → better future search, lore, games, and edit inventory
```

Prompts and layouts can be copied. Rebuilding the audited evidence, aliases,
corrections, creator vocabulary, evaluation questions, and connected history
requires time and domain judgment.

## Universal formula

The Memory OS is reusable because its evidence contract stays stable while its
channel DNA changes.

For WWAM, the native objects are franchises, takes, recurring characters,
dark-comedy categories, commentaries, and livestreams. For a racing league,
they become seasons, races, drivers, lead changes, close finishes, booth
intensity, and recurring broadcast moments. The system is portable; the
personality and proof are not generic.
